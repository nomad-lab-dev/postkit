How to choose —

V3a Editorial Discovery · poetic, curated, slow burn. Resonates with the landing's existing Instrument Serif × orange aesthetic. Best if your audience is craft-oriented (writers, photographers, designers).

V3b Bold Tech · punchy, declarative, numbers as proof. Closer to Linear / NB Camera. Best for indie devs, power users, and the "build in public" crowd. Strongest for short-form Reels/TikTok demos.

V3c Warm Personal · friendly, conversational, like a smart friend speaking. Closer to BeReal / Headspace / Calm. Best for habit-formation positioning and casual creators. Plays well in App Store screenshots that need to feel human.

All three share the exact same 5-step structure — the only thing that changes is voice + palette + typography. Once you pick, I wire it up in SwiftUI and we can A/B the copy on the landing too.
