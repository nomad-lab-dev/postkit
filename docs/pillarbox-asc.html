App Store description (4 000 chars max)

PostKit turns your camera roll into daily content. You have 15,000 photos. PostKit classifies them by content pillar on-device — Travel, Food, Build in public, whatever you want to be known for. Then Smart Post drafts a finished post in one prompt: "Make a post about my Italy trip" → 6 photos, 6 cities, a ready-to-share carousel. WHY POSTKIT • Your camera roll is the best content you'll never post — until now • On-device classification: photos never leave your phone unless you decide • 30 seconds a day, every morning. That's the entire deal. CORE FEATURES • Smart classification — Core ML on-device + Gemini Flash fallback when uncertain • Content Pillars — declare 2-7 things you want to be known for • Smart Post — type a prompt, get a finished post draft with photos + caption • Templates — reusable formats (Italian Summer, Weekly Cars, Build Logs) • Scheduled posting — pick weekdays, the right post drops into your queue • Privacy-first — Apple-style on-device by default, cloud opt-in DESIGNED FOR DAILY CREATORS Whether you're an indie founder posting #buildinpublic, a coffee shop owner with too many product shots, or a traveler whose Instagram lags 6 months behind reality — PostKit cuts the gap between "I should post" and "I posted" from 30 minutes to 30 seconds. PRO SUBSCRIPTION (optional) • Unlimited Smart Post generations • Cloud-assisted classification for ambiguous photos • Multi-platform tone (Instagram / LinkedIn / X) • Priority support 9.99 €/mo · cancel anytime · 7-day free trial PRIVACY Photos stay on-device. Pillars and templates sync via iCloud if you opt in. Optional Gemini Flash fallback only sends low-resolution thumbnails for ambiguous photos, and only with your explicit consent. Built native in SwiftUI with on-device Core ML. Made in France.
